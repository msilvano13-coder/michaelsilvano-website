Privacy Policy

Effective June 12, 2026

Michael Silvano Photography ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit michaelsilvano.com or get in touch, how we use it, and the choices you have.

01 Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you submit an inquiry or contact form, book a session, or email us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and details about your event or project — such as the date, location, type of session, and any message you send. If you become a client, we may also collect information needed to deliver and bill for our services.

Information collected automatically

When you browse our website, we and our service providers may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages you view, and the site or ad that referred you. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies (see below).

02 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and tracking pixels to operate the site, understand how visitors use it, and measure and improve our advertising. In particular, we use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook and Instagram). The Meta Pixel helps us understand the actions people take on our site, show relevant ads to people who have visited us, and measure how well our ads perform.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage how Meta uses your data for advertising in your Facebook ad preferences and Instagram settings.

03 How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you about your event or project
  • To provide, schedule, deliver, and invoice for our photography services
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • To market our services, including showing and measuring ads on platforms like Facebook and Instagram
  • To comply with our legal obligations and protect our rights

04 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business, including with:

  • Service providers that help us operate — for example, our email, customer-management and booking, website hosting, and payment-processing partners — who handle your information on our behalf.
  • Advertising and analytics partners, such as Meta, that help us deliver and measure ads. These partners process limited information according to their own privacy policies.
  • Legal and safety recipients, when required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.
  • A successor in the event of a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets.

05 Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and meet our legal, tax, and recordkeeping obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

06 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain uses such as targeted advertising. Residents of California and certain other states have these rights under applicable law. To make a request, email us at the address below — we will respond as required by law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

07 Your Choices

  • Adjust or block cookies through your browser settings
  • Manage ad personalization in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising via the Digital Advertising Alliance
  • Unsubscribe from our emails using the link in any message, or by contacting us

08 Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites and services we do not control. This policy does not apply to those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

09 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10 Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Material changes will be posted on this page.

12 Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at michael@michaelsilvano.com.
