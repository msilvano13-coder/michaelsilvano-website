Meghan & Pat · A North Shore Garden Wedding

On Saturday, August 30, 2025, Meghan and Pat were married at Floriana in Ipswich, Massachusetts, a garden venue on Boston's North Shore. Meghan got ready at the Essex Street Inn in Newburyport before a first look, and the day leaned soft and classic: neutrals, whites and creams and greens, with the bridesmaids in butter yellow. The ceremony unfolded outdoors beneath a sprawling old tree, down an aisle lined with hydrangeas.

About 115 guests gathered for golden-hour portraits in the gardens, a candlelit dinner, and heartfelt toasts, then filled the dance floor well into the night. Captured on both film and digital, Meghan and Pat's wedding closed with a glowing send-off under string lights, an easy, joyful North Shore celebration.
