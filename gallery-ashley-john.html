Ashley & John — An Oceanfront Wedding at The Chanler

On Saturday, October 18, 2025, Ashley and John were married at The Chanler at Cliff Walk in Newport, Rhode Island — the oceanfront boutique hotel perched at the head of Newport's famed Cliff Walk, overlooking the Atlantic. In a palette of black, white, and sage green, the day began with a first look before an autumn ceremony with the sea as a backdrop.

Around 130 guests moved from cocktails to a four-course dinner with live music — three bands across the night, including a baby grand at dinner — while the Cliff Walk glowed gold at sunset for portraits. The dance floor stayed full to the last song before Ashley and John left for a honeymoon in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, closing one of the most elegant Newport weddings of the season.
