Caite & Sean · A Newport Summer Wedding

On Friday, June 19, 2026, Caite and Sean were married in Newport, Rhode Island, a classic summer celebration in black, white, and greenery. The day began with the bridal party getting ready at the Newport Beach Hotel & Suites, then moved to a candlelit Catholic ceremony at Our Lady of Mercy Chapel at Salve Regina, beneath soaring wood beams and stained glass. In a tradition the couple held close, Sean walked his mother down the aisle, and Caite carried her late grandmother's veil, small details that made the morning feel like family.

After a ribbon-wand send-off on the chapel steps, the celebration moved to OceanCliff, the oceanfront mansion on Newport's Ridge Road. Golden-hour portraits unfolded on the lawn above Narragansett Bay, and roughly 160 guests filled a ballroom of floor-to-ceiling windows for cocktails, dinner, and a first dance to "Home." The night ended the way the best ones do, a packed floor, glow sticks in the air, and a couple who never stopped smiling.
