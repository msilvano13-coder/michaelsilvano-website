Questions

Before you ask

Is this a photo booth?

No. There is no kiosk, screen or prop table. It is a small portrait studio run by a photographer who directs every sitting, the way a studio session would be.

Do I need to hire Michael as my wedding photographer?

No. RSVP Portraits can be booked on its own alongside any photographer, or added to a Michael Silvano Photography wedding collection.

How do we get the portraits?

Every portrait is edited and delivered in an online gallery within a week. You can share the gallery with your guests so they can find themselves.

Does it work for events other than weddings?

Yes. The studio suits galas, fundraisers, milestone parties and corporate events as well as wedding receptions.
