About the evening

Held at the Hyatt Regency Greenwich in Connecticut, Largay Travel's Connect 2026 brought advisors, partners, and supplier friends together for a night of recognition and celebration. As the event photographer, my goal was to document the evening the way it actually felt — the red-carpet portraits, the candid laughter, the toasts on stage, and the details the team worked so hard to get right. Coverage like this is built for the way organizations use it afterward: press recaps, partner thank-yous, social posts, and next year's invitation.
