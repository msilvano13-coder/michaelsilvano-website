Chris & Abby · A Gloucester Harbor Wedding

On Friday, July 3, 2026, Chris and Abby were married in Gloucester, Massachusetts, a harbor town wedding from start to finish. The morning began at the Beauport Hotel, where Abby and her bridesmaids got ready with hair and makeup by The Bridal Suite, a blue-and-white palette running through everything from the invitation suite and pearls to the bridesmaids' matching sets. After a first look with her father, Abby stepped onto a white trolley bound for the church.

The ceremony was held at Our Lady of Good Voyage, Gloucester's storied fishermen's church, beneath its gold altar arch and the Madonna cradling a schooner. A "Just Married" trolley ride later, the celebration moved to the water for a cocktail hour cruise on Gloucester Harbor, schooners under sail in the distance, before docking at Cruiseport Gloucester for dinner and dancing. Toasts, a hydrangea-topped cake, and parent dances gave way to cowboy hats, a short white party dress for Abby, and a dance floor that carried the night into the Fourth of July weekend.

What is Cruiseport Gloucester?

Cruiseport Gloucester is a waterfront wedding and event venue on Gloucester's inner harbor, with a grand ballroom, harbor views, and dockside access that lets couples pair their reception with a cocktail hour cruise on Gloucester Harbor.
