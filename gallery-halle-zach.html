Halle & Zach — A Newport Mansion Wedding

On Sunday, July 19, 2026, Halle and Zach were married in Newport, Rhode Island, a full summer day that moved through the city's most iconic settings. The morning began at a shingled cottage near the harbor, where Halle got ready with her bridesmaids, a first look with her father on the porch, and happy tears all around before the trolley departed. At The Elms, the Bellevue Avenue mansion, Zach turned for their first look at the marble teahouse, followed by family formals, wedding party portraits, and a kiss on the great lawn with the mansion rising behind them.

The ceremony was held on the Bridge Lawn at OceanCliff, beneath a flower-topped pergola with Narragansett Bay glittering behind and the bride's brother officiating to live ceremony music. Oysters and cocktails followed on the tented Upper Deck, then dinner beneath the chandeliers of the bay-view ballroom, toasts, parent dances, and a golden hour slipped away on the lawn. A terrace cake cutting, college flags on the dance floor, and a ribbon-wand sendoff carried the newlyweds into the Newport night.
