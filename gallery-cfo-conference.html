CFO Leadership Conference

Sheraton Boston · Spring 2026 · Boston, Massachusetts

Two days with the CFO Leadership Council at the Sheraton Boston — keynotes, breakout sessions, the exhibit hall, and the conversations between finance leaders that the conference is really built for. Full coverage of a packed Grand Ballroom and everything happening around it.

About the conference

Held over two days at the Sheraton Boston, the Spring 2026 CFO Leadership Conference brought hundreds of finance executives together for keynotes, breakout sessions, and roundtables on the issues shaping the office of the CFO — from AI in finance to navigating economic uncertainty. As the event photographer for the CFO Leadership Council, my job was to document the conference the way it actually felt: a full Grand Ballroom, engaged audiences, real conversations in the exhibit hall, and the speakers and members at the center of it. Conference coverage like this is built for the way an organization uses it afterward — recap decks, sponsor and partner thank-yous, social and press, and the marketing for next year's event.
