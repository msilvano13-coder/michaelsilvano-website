An evening on behalf of the Peabody Essex Museum, hosted in the airy light of Skylight Studios. From the first arrivals to the last toast, the night unfolded with warmth, conversation, and the kind of details worth remembering.

About the evening

Held at Skylight Studios in Woburn, Massachusetts, this gathering on behalf of the Peabody Essex Museum brought supporters together for a night of connection and celebration. As the event photographer, my goal was simple: document the room as it actually felt — the candid laughter, the quiet moments between conversations, and the details the team worked so hard to get right. Coverage like this is built for the way organizations use it afterward: press recaps, sponsor thank-yous, social posts, and next year's invitation.
