Gabi & Ike · A Botanical Garden Wedding in Providence

On Sunday, August 31, 2025, Gabi and Ike were married inside the Roger Williams Park Botanical Center in Providence, Rhode Island, the largest indoor public garden in the state, all glass walls and towering green. Their palette leaned tropical-but-cool, pinks and greens and blues, and the day opened with a first look before a ceremony among the palms. Gabi wore an engagement ring that once belonged to Ike's grandmother, a quiet piece of family woven through the day.

Roughly 160 guests filled the greenhouse for cocktails, dinner, and toasts, then took to a dance floor that never really emptied, all of it captured on both film and digital, the way the night actually felt. When the last song played at the garden, the celebration kept right on going at Providence's Hot Club. A lush, joyful, distinctly Rhode Island wedding.
